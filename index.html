Design basis

The concept retains the two outdoor living levels and develops a rectangular Unit 1 pool and a splayed Unit 2 pool. The Unit 2 west edge is inset to make room for a 1.50m stair bay. The original red sketch is retained in the detailed drawing.

The source identifies a 2.44m rear drainage and sewerage easement, a 2,743mm Melbourne Water asset and a separate 225mm stormwater pipe. Surveyed offsets and depths remain to be confirmed. Pool excavation intersects source tree protection rings; arborist and authority review is required.

Supplied photo-style images describe material and visual intent. Native CAD and Blender views carry the geometry. Five 200mm concept risers require detailed stair and barrier design approval.
